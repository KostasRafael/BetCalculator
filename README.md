# Bet Calculator Web App

## The bet Calculator provides usefull information and statistics about football

You can open the deployed Bet Calculator at: `<insert url>`. From there, you can choose the league you are interested in looking up. Currently there are 4 leagues available: English Premier League, Greek Superleague, German Bundesliga, and Spanish La Liga. Once you have chosen the league, you can see the upcoming fixtures in that league. You can click on the fixture you want to study, and see relevant information such as the results of the last 10 matches for each team in the fixture, the average goals scored by each team, the probability of a home team win, a draw, or an away team win, and the probability of over/under goals.

The BetCalculator front-end was built using html, css and React. Football data is fetched from the API-Football at https://www.api-football.com/.
